### The Honest Operator Challenge: Reality Over Hype

Social media feeds are full of claims that "AI does 100% of the work with one click." Here is the grounded reality from an active operator:

> **AI agents still cannot operate at the domain expertise and nuanced intuition needed for autonomous public-facing work.**

* **The Hallucination Trap:** Models love to guess unit economics, invent competitor margins, or gloss over edge-case financial realities unless bound by strict verification rules.
* **Supervision & Steering:** A significant portion of my time is still invested in writing precise specifications, reviewing diffs, verifying data sources against raw receipts, and correcting subtle logical drifts.
* **Verification Over Generation:** High-stakes decisions demand zero fabricated progress. The value is not in letting AI run wild; it is in using AI as a relentless force multiplier under expert human guidance.
